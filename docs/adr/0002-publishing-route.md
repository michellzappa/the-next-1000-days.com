# ADR 0002: Publishing route

Status: proposed, MZ decides

## Options

1. **Self-publish, print on demand** (for example Leanpub for the ebook and KDP or IngramSpark for print). Fast, keeps rights and pricing, own ISBN.
2. **Small independent publisher** in the Netherlands or Brazil. Slower, adds editing and distribution, shares rights.
3. **Free ebook plus paid print.** Maximum reach for the ideas, the print edition is the credential.

## Recommendation

Option 1, with the ebook priced low. It is the fastest route to "author of", and option 2 stays open for the Portuguese edition.

## To decide

Route, price, and whether to buy an own ISBN or use the platform's.
